# Development journal

## Stage 1 — direct output was not true pixel art

High-resolution pseudo-pixel images looked convincing at display size, but naïve downscaling blurred eyes, facial marks, hair breaks, and costume emblems. The first lesson was to recover a lattice rather than shrink a picture.

## Stage 2 — the second resize destroyed recovered detail

The v6 turnaround recovered logical pixels and then resized the recovered character again to normalize height. That second transformation erased exactly the features recovery was supposed to protect. V7 removed post-recovery resizing and placed recovered pixels `1:1` on the final canvas.

## Stage 3 — eight independent views needed topology

Generating eight directions separately preserved detail but created changes in head size, body mass, anchors, and palette. The V8 turnaround introduced a south identity authority, explicit anchor cards, cardinal-first generation, diagonal dual-neighbor constraints, a canonical palette, and circular direction review.

V8 became the first approved eight-direction idle gold.

## Stage 4 — a single animation sheet was too convenient

The first walk experiments asked image generation for a multi-frame sheet. It produced perspective changes, duplicate poses, inconsistent scale, and weak phase meaning. The pipeline switched to one complete high-detail generation per phase.

## Stage 5 — numerical symmetry hid broken anatomy

V2 mirrored motion pixels and restored identity with a broad mask. Its cadence ratios became perfectly symmetric, yet 16x review exposed detached arms, missing forearms, and seams through shoulders, waist, hips, and legs. Green metrics were not visual correctness.

## Stage 6 — local discrete reasoning

The final process kept separately generated complete bodies and applied bounded exact postprocessing only to stable identity blocks. The complete same-view head and cross emblem were stored as exact RGBA cell sets, including transparent erase cells and context rings. Articulated features stayed phase-specific.

This reduced canonical head and emblem mismatch to zero without resampling or changing pixels outside approved coordinates.

## Stage 7 — diagnostics stopped pretending to be verdicts

The approved walk retained a paired silhouette-change ratio above an early reference limit. Normal-speed and slow-loop review nevertheless showed a coherent chibi walk, and the user approved it as perfect. The pipeline therefore separated hard integrity gates from visual diagnostics: the warning remains recorded, but subjective motion is decided through explicit visual authority.

## Current result

- eight-direction `64×64` idle temporal/spatial baseline;
- six-frame `64×64` south walk temporal gold;
- deterministic recovery and audit scripts;
- exact local pixel workbench;
- failures retained as regression fixtures instead of deleted history.
