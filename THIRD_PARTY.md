# Third-party tools and services

## Perfect Pixel

- Project: [theamusing/perfectPixel](https://github.com/theamusing/perfectPixel)
- Version used by the recorded pipeline: `perfect-pixel==0.1.4`
- License: MIT
- Role: recovery of logical cells from reviewed high-resolution pseudo-pixel lattices using majority sampling.
- Distribution: not vendored in this repository; installed as a runtime dependency by the scripts.

## OpenAI ImageGen

OpenAI ImageGen was used to create high-detail and pseudo-pixel raster sources. Image generation is a service step and is not vendored or reproduced by the deterministic scripts in this repository.

## Excluded source material

The Village_Rim Unity project, original third-party art packs, pose-reference sprites, and unapproved enemy prototypes are deliberately excluded from this publication.
